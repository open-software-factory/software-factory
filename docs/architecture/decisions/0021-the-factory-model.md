# 0021: The factory model

Status: accepted

Date: 2026-10-21

## Context

The factory had no single description of its parts, its actors and the develop and review jobs. The terms and decisions were spread across the decision records, the vision, the development guide and the older design documents, and they contradicted each other.

## Decision

The model is [the factory model](../factory-model.md). It carries the purpose, the terms, the parts and ownership, who calls whom, the develop job, the review job, the journal, identities and credentials, checks and risk, and configuration and adoption. The decisions that need their own record stay in their records and are mapped from the model.

## Consequences

The model is the one description a reader starts from. A decision that changes the model changes the model document and its record. The vocabulary is the Terms table in the model, and the older vocabulary document is retired.
